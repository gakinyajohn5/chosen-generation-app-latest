Welcome to Chosen Gen Hub

A quick guide to installing the app and using its features

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install to confirm.
  5. Find the Chosen Gen Hub icon on your home screen — open it any time like a normal app.
  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome — Add to Home Screen only works from Safari on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. Find the Chosen Gen Hub icon on your home screen — open it any time like a normal app.

Once installed, the app opens full-screen with no browser bar, and works even with a weak connection for pages you've already visited.
